Our point of view

Frameworks are our toolkit, not our religion.

Most consultancies arrive with one framework and fit your organisation to it. We start from how your organisation funds, delivers and runs change, then select and adapt proven practices for each part of it. Projects, products and live services each get the way of working that suits their responsibilities, and the model connects them.

We are not tied to, or affiliated with, any framework owner.

  • Lean thinkingValue, flow and waste removal
  • KanbanVisual flow, WIP limits, flow metrics
  • ScrumSprints, Product Owner, Definition of Done
  • Lean portfolio managementFund products, not projects
  • User-centred designResearch and testing with users
  • DevSecOpsSmall, safe, automated releases
  • ITIL 4Change, requests, incidents, problems
  • PRINCE2Stages, gates and project boards
  • Safety and complianceHazard logs and safety sign-off
  • OKRsShort-cycle objectives and key results
  • Cost of delayPrioritise by the cost of waiting
  • Benefits realisationBaselines, profiles and tracking
  • SAFeOnly where several organisations share a roadmap

✓ Recognised by Atlassian as a verified solution, built specifically to deliver healthcare product change safely and improve operational and financial efficiency.

DelPulse is Agile Elite's product operating model and the platform that runs it. It gives projects, products and live services each the way of working that suits them, and connects them, so leadership, the PMO, programmes, product teams and service teams all work from the same picture. Healthcare is where it is proven today; the model is configured for any complex organisation.

Projects

A business case, approval gates, go-live and stabilisation, aligned with PRINCE2. At gate G5 open work, actions and benefits pass to a named product owner and team.

Products

Product teams take demand through one front door, order it by value and safety, and improve continuously in Scrum or Kanban.

Live services

Service teams work from a queue, aligned with ITIL 4, with routes for requests, incidents, emergencies and recurring problems.

  • Start small, add layersEssential for one to three product teams, then Organisation, Network for several organisations sharing a roadmap, and Portfolio for funding decisions across them.
  • Twelve practices as leversLean, Kanban, Scrum, lean portfolio management, user-centred design, DevSecOps, ITIL 4, safety and compliance, OKRs, cost of delay and benefits realisation, with SAFe used only for coordination at network scale.
  • Governance built in27 roles, gates the platform checks, records with approval and a four-eyes rule, run-in-the-platform events, and an audit trail that cannot be edited.
  • Its own work trackerCapabilities, epics, features and stories with boards, sprints and a value index, or an Atlassian backbone where Jira and Confluence already run delivery.

FitB · fitb.social

FitB: nobody gets fit alone.

FitB is a social fitness platform that pairs members with workout buddies, live classes and personal trainers, with an AI coach that keeps people going between sessions. It runs on the web and as an iOS app, in English and Spanish.

Visit fitb.social
For members

Buddies and weekly pacts

Members who opt in are paired with someone on the same journey, and the pair sets a weekly pact that renews while both keep showing up.

Four journeys

Weight loss, muscle, fitness or wellbeing. Every feed, video and programme is ordered for the member's own journey.

Live classes and 1:1 calls

Camera-and-audio sessions with trainers and buddies, all in one shared room.

FitB Crew (AI)

An AI buddy, always labelled as AI, that builds walking, workout and meal plans on request, logs what you tell it and links you to the exact screen.

The scale is not the score

Weight is shown as a smoothed trend with a daily score, so one bad weigh-in never reads as failure.

FitNote and Weight Loss hub

A members' feed for progress posts, plus a curated hub of free, approved clips and programmes.

For trainers and gyms

Trainer marketplace

Verified trainers publish a business page, sell sessions, classes and challenges, and get paid with clear VAT receipts.

Gyms

Gym owners list their gym, classes and memberships under a standard agreement, with FitB acting as their commercial agent.

Business onboarding

Trainers and owners get their own Crew guide with set-up check-ins and a free setup call.

How it is built

Rules live in the system, not just the screen.

  • Security in the databaseBookings, payments and messages are checked by database rules, so hiding a button is never the only protection.
  • Honest AIA second model reviews every Crew reply for accuracy, safety and tone before it is sent. Doubtful replies go to a person.
  • Measured AIEvery model call is logged and evaluated, with a path to FitB's own models earned through shadow and canary testing.
  • Two languagesEvery member-facing line is translated to Spanish, enforced by an automated check on every change.
  • Privacy firstAI training data is used only with member consent, set under Settings > Privacy.
Undeniably Good Co. · coming soon

Real creators. Real views. Undeniably good. Coming soon

A UK-first creator marketplace. Brands fund campaigns, vetted creators make short videos, and creators are paid per video plus per 1,000 verified views. Launching in fitness and wellness, with FitB as the first brand.

Creators keep 100%

Guaranteed pay is reserved from the brand's wallet when a creator joins a campaign. Earnings clear after 7 days and are paid every Thursday from £20.

No subscription for brands

Top up a wallet from £100, pay creator earnings plus a platform fee, set budget caps, and withdraw what you don't spend. Usage rights on your own channels are included.

Every view is real

Views are read from the platforms for 30 days. Suspicious spikes are held, and both sides see the same evidence.

Vetted creators

Account checks plus a human review of sample videos. AI recommends, people decide, and craft is the only thing scored.

Undeniably Good Guarantee

Free changes, and if a video still misses the brief the brand doesn't pay and is offered another vetted creator.

UK compliant by design

Built around ASA and CMA rules and the DMCC Act, with the exact approved video published from the app.

  1. Apply

    Creators connect their accounts and share sample videos for review.

  2. Match

    Brands launch a campaign and are matched with suitable creators.

  3. Draft and review

    The brand approves or requests changes within 48 hours.

  4. Post and get paid

    The creator posts from the app, views are tracked, and pay goes out weekly.

Challenge

EPR improvement requests arrived by spreadsheet, email and meeting. Nobody could see total demand, what mattered most, or what each change was worth.

Approach

We designed the product operating model and then ran it: one front door for demand, owned backlogs, prioritisation on clinical safety, operational and financial impact, two-weekly sprints and benefits tracking. We led the PAS, Clinical Documentation, Urgent & Emergency Care and EPMA product teams, built the investment case and the shared EMAP governance.

Impact

  • 183 optimisation items delivered
  • 848 requests in one prioritised backlog; monthly intake up from 8 to 47
  • Proven at Leicester and Northamptonshire, now scaling to all 8 trusts

The Agile Elite differenceWe designed the model and ran the teams, so it was proven on live EPR work before it scaled. It is now packaged as DelPulse, so every trust can adopt it without starting again.
